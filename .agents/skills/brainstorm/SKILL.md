# RPGlitch Brainstorm & Proposals

> **Non-Canon Boundary**: Nothing in `references/` is implemented or guaranteed. Treat all files here as exploratory proposals until explicitly promoted to [ROADMAP.md](../../../ROADMAP.md).

## Workflow

1. **Browse**: List or read proposals in `references/` when exploring a new feature area.
2. **Evaluate**: Check alignment with [README.md](../../../README.md) and `DESIGN.md` before committing to scope.
3. **Promote**: When an idea is approved, add it to [ROADMAP.md](../../../ROADMAP.md) and archive or remove the draft from `references/`.
